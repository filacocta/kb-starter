# Ideas Backlog

Durable parking lot for broad side-project ideas captured through `readinglist/topics.md` or similar inboxes. Items here are not auto-ingested. They are periodically triaged into projects, topic research ideas, reading-list sources, or explicit skips.

Use `_tools/kb-checkoff.py --route-ideas-backlog` when checking off future inspiration-capture lines so the routed item lands here under the KB write lock.

## Review Rules

- Convert implementation ideas into `projects/<slug>/_summary.md` + `_todos.md`.
- Convert research domains into `topics/<slug>/research-ideas.md` or exact URLs in `topics/<slug>/reading-list.md`.
- Leave speculative, unsourced, cross-topic ideas here until there is enough shape to promote.
- Never write source claims here; source-grounded knowledge belongs in topics.
