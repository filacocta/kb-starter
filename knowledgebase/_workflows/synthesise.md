# Article maintenance

Read the [write contract](../_rules.md), the target article and relevant inspected
sources. A whole-topic request may read the whole topic; an incremental update
does not. Retrieve related project context only when it affects the requested
conclusions or actions. No compulsory global index scan or universal action ledger.

Improve the existing narrative before adding pages. Remove repetition and
reorganize mutable prose when it improves understanding; append-only growth is
not required. Preserve useful evidence, human annotations, disagreements and
the rationale of substantive corrections. Published sources remain unchanged.
One useful source may justify an article; source counts and word quotas do not
require stubs, new topics or a page per concept.

Cite material claims near their source evidence. Distinguish an attributed
claim, an inspected observation and cross-source inference. Preserve evidence
quality, source dependence and unresolved conflict instead of manufacturing
consensus. A saved answer is a dated derived view, not new independent evidence.

For reviewed sources, reuse the article's Sources or Review section to record
incorporated, no change or deferred with source link, date, reason and inspected
hash when practical. No material contribution needs no generated page.

For health, financial, safety or speculative forecasting conclusions, check
primary/guideline evidence and seek independent review proportionate to impact.
Anecdotes, marketing, mechanistic plausibility, demos and synthetic tests do not
establish clinical advice, investment performance, safe installation or forecast
skill. Keep unsupported claims attributed and bounded. Model recommendations
distinguish documentation, local observations, evaluations and experiment.

Extract actions only when useful to the requested task. Store a clear task,
evidence and unresolved gate at an existing destination: exact URL to Pending,
research question to research-ideas, project task to its board, book to its
ledger, or KB/tooling work to `_todo.md`. Use the existing queue/ledger tools.
A possible recommendation does not authorize fetching, installation or execution.
Defer ambiguous candidates without blocking unrelated authorized article edits.

Update affected navigation, run scoped checks and record the completed mutation
once. Ordinary article maintenance needs no phase approval menu or per-action
checkpoint. Use a scoped resumable record only when the operation actually needs it.
